Frequently Asked Questions

What is AfriVid Studio?
AfriVid Studio is Africa's first AI video creation platform. It allows anyone to create professional videos, edit footage, translate voice to African languages, generate slides and design graphics — all from a browser, free.
Is AfriVid Studio free?
Yes. AfriVid Studio is free during beta. The free plan includes 3 AI videos per month, AI editor sessions, voice translation and unlimited photo and design tools. Pro plan is $5/month.
What languages does AfriVid support?
AfriVid Studio supports 6 African languages: Swahili, Yoruba, Hausa, French, Arabic and Portuguese. You can create and dub videos in these languages natively.
How do I create a video with AfriVid?
Type your topic, choose a video style and language, then click Generate. AfriVid writes the script, generates voice, builds slides and exports a complete MP4 video automatically.
Can I upload to YouTube directly from AfriVid?
Yes. AfriVid Studio has a one-click YouTube upload feature. After generating your video, click Upload to YouTube, sign in with your Google account and the video uploads directly to your channel.
