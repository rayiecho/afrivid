Pricing

Pay for what you actually make.

AfriVid runs on credits. Credits buy generated video, and one credit means the same thing on every plan. Everything below is what you get — including what stops working when your free allowance is gone.

The rate, in full
1 minute of generated video = 16 credits

Charged by the second on the finished video, not on what you asked for — a 90‑second video costs 24 credits, not a rounded-up minute. The same rate applies on every plan.

When your free allowance runs out

Nothing here is hidden in a footnote. This is exactly what the free account does once the credits are spent and the trial windows have closed — and exactly what a $1 unlock or Pro turns back on.

The free account after its allowance

Your account keeps working and your files stay yours. What changes is what the tools will do:

No new video can be generated. Ad Maker, Tutorial Maker and Short Videos stop.
A watermark appears on video you export.
Auto-captions stop working — including cut-by-caption.
The mic overlay is disabled.
Noise reduction is capped at its lowest level only.
Volume boost is capped at its lowest level only.
The Developer API is not accessible.
Compression still works — at low quality, watermarked.
